“Can I just do it myself?”

Yes, you can.

ChatGPT

It'll write you a page. It won't buy your domain, hook it up, host it, make the button work, or tell you your bio sounds like a robot. I use the same tools. You get one finished thing instead of a chat window and a to-do list.

Squarespace or Wix

Fine if you enjoy dragging boxes around. Most people don't. You pay them forever, it looks like everyone else's, and at midnight when something breaks there's nobody to message. With me, there's me.
